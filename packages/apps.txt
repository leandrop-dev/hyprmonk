# The apps the installer offers to pick (install.sh apps, or the checklist in
# its menu); the rest of the desktop, in pacman.txt and aur.txt, always comes.
# One app per line: its name in the checklist | its packages, from the repos
# or the AUR (paru installs both; CachyOS's own repo first) | what it is.
# The picks are kept in ~/.config/hyprmonk/apps; without that file, all.

Zen Browser | zen-browser-bin | the browser; opens web links
mpv | mpv mpv-mpris | video and audio, seen by the media panel
Papers | papers | PDFs and other documents
Zed | zed | text and code; opens text files
Calculator | gnome-calculator | GNOME's calculator
btop | btop | system monitor in the terminal
Resources | resources | system monitor, with the GPU
Partition Manager | partitionmanager | disks and partitions (KDE)
Filelight | filelight | what fills a disk (KDE)
Archive tools | unzip zip 7zip unrar | zip, 7z and RAR, in the terminal and in Nautilus
LocalSend | localsend | sharing files with nearby devices
GPU Screen Recorder | gpu-screen-recorder | screen recording
Deskflow | deskflow | keyboard and mouse shared with other computers
Printing | cups cups-pk-helper system-config-printer | printers, network ones too
Flatpak | flatpak | apps from Flathub
Firmware updates | fwupd | firmware from the vendors (LVFS)
English spelling | hunspell-en_us | spell checking in English (US)
Portuguese spelling | hunspell-pt_pt | spell checking in Portuguese (Portugal)
